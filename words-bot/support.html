Getting started

Meet Wordy, a robot who's really good at Wordle. Play his games, numbered from #1 and the same for everyone, and ask him for help when you want it: the words that still fit, his suggestions with their numbers, or his pick. Watch him play your next game or one you've played, with his reason for every guess. In Strategies, make him play your way, test your strategy on 2,000 games and compare it with his. Stats shows your games, his, and how you compare. The ? button explains every part.

Questions

Will watching Wordy spoil a game?

Watching your next game shows its answer and spends it, so he first offers to let you play it. Games you've played are yours to watch any time.

Why wasn't my word accepted?

Wordy's word list is close to Wordle's but may not match it exactly. A word he doesn't know shakes the row, as in Wordle. So does a guess that breaks Hard Mode's rule, with a note saying what's missing.

Does it need the internet?

No. Every game and word is in the app. iCloud only syncs your own games between your devices.

My games aren't on my other device.

Check that each device is signed in to the same iCloud account, with iCloud turned on for the app. Syncing can take a few minutes.

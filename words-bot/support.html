Build a Words Bot Support

A Wordle™ robot you build, learn from and play against: a fun extra for your daily game, not a replacement for it.

Need help, found a problem, or have an idea? Email tutorapps@icloud.com. Please include your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac), the day you were playing or watching, and Wordy's strategy if it's about him.

Getting started

Meet Wordy, a robot who's really good at Wordle. Play today's game or any past day, and ask him for help when you want it: the words that still fit, his suggestions with their numbers, or his pick. Watch him play any past day, with his reason for every guess. In Strategies, make him play your way, test your strategy on past days and compare it with his. Stats shows your games, his, and how you compare. The ? button explains every part.

Questions

Will Wordy spoil today's game?

No. He plays today's game only after you've finished it, and today's answer stays hidden until then.

Why wasn't my word accepted?

Wordy's word list is close to Wordle's but may not match it exactly. A word he doesn't know shakes the row, as in Wordle. So does a guess that breaks Hard Mode's rule, with a note saying what's missing.

Why don't past answers show up yet?

The app gets past answers over the internet, through iCloud. Offline, past days still play, but today waits for its answer. Once you're back online they arrive on their own.

My games aren't on my other device.

Check that each device is signed in to the same iCloud account, with iCloud turned on for the app. Syncing can take a few minutes.

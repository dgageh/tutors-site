Privacy Policy

Build a Words Bot · Effective October 26, 2026

In short: Build a Words Bot doesn't collect any information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your games and strategies stay on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account, where only you can see them — we can't.

Information we collect

None. Build a Words Bot does not collect, share or sell any personal information or usage data. The app contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics or tracking. Its only network connections are Apple's iCloud: to sync your own data between your devices. Its games and words are all in the app.

What the app keeps on your devices

  • Settings: Hard Mode, which strategy Wordy plays, whether he knows past answers, whether he offers to let you play a game first, and which place and level of help you used last.
  • Your games: for each game you finish, its number, its answer, your guesses, whether you solved it, whether Hard Mode was on, how much help you asked Wordy for on each guess, and whether it was a replay or played after watching him.
  • Wordy's games: the games you watch him play, with his guesses, the game's number and the strategy he played.
  • Your strategies and their tests: each strategy's name and skills, and the results when you test one: how many guesses each game took.

Deleting the app deletes everything it saved on that device.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, the app keeps your games, Wordy's games, your strategies, their tests and your settings in sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage), in your own private iCloud account. Only you can read it: it's stored under Apple's privacy policy, and we can't see it. To keep everything on one device, turn off iCloud for the app in your device's iCloud settings.

Crash reports from Apple

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. Apple controls this, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements (on a Mac, System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements).

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date.
